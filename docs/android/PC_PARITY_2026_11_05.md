# KytyPS5 Plus Android — focused PC parity and failure review (2026-11-05)

## Diagnostics, 06:49 local

- PPSA03671 (Wolverine): abort in `descriptorHeap.cpp:62` at `!Allocate(layout, fresh_batch)` after 22 compute shaders. 934 MiB GPU usage against 4608 MiB budget; zero evictions. The Android pool declares storage buffers, sampled images, storage images and samplers, but `NativeDescriptorType(UniformBuffers)` emits `eUniformBuffer`. Jetsku/KytyPS5 reserves 4096 uniform descriptors. Port that declaration only; do **not** scale all Vulkan descriptor pools 4x on ARM64. Failure mode is consistent with missing allocation capacity, not proof of shader incompatibility recovery. CI contract check guards this.
- PPSA01341 (Demon's Souls): 52 guest flips, then 4, then many 5-second windows with zero; last suspended guest points 78; VS 6 / PS 15 / CS 177. VRAM 3029 of 4608 MiB, zero evictions. Guest ceased sending new flips; presentation cannot repair missing guest work. HLE ring shows AudioOut2 PortSetAttributes and cond timed waits; these may be normal loops and do **not** establish a root cause. Avoid FPS claims or forced render/sync bypass.
- User-tested binary identifies commit `d1df9ce9` (2026-11-04), preceding the newer `experiment/demons-souls-nexus-route` option. That branch adds reversible, opt-in `FullGame` + `cp11_debug_forceOptionToSkipTutorial`. This mirrors the PC report [KytyPS5 issue #1461](https://github.com/KytyPS5/KytyPS5/issues/1461), which states the Journey fog freezes but skipping to the Nexus works there. **Not confirmed on Android.**
